# Code of conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds the project:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit
messages — and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately, through the same channel the security
policy names: a GitHub private advisory at
<https://github.com/tanyu216/meerkat-taskpanel/security/advisories/new>. It is private, and it
reaches only the maintainer.

Reports are read promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obliged to respect the privacy and
security of the person reporting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides the response, in proportion to the behaviour and its history:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining the problem and why it was inappropriate, possibly
   asking for a public apology.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a period of no
   interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.

A maintainer who does not follow this code in good faith may face consequences decided by the
project's other maintainers; where there is only one maintainer, the outcome is recorded in the
decision that resolves the report.
