# The routing decision

An unowned card is the route decision: assign exactly one accountable role from the routing
table the board supplies. Choose only from the **discovered role catalog** — its `shapes` are
the routing table's source of truth; a work shape the table does not describe goes to the
**Worker** role; never invent a role name. A reviewer-lane role is never routed an ordinary
card. The card's assignee still selects the executing role — this table routes, it never
decides. An already-assigned card is not yours to re-route; leave a fresh or ambiguous card
for its creator.
