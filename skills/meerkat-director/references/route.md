# The routing decision

An unowned card is the route decision: assign exactly one accountable role from the routing
table the board supplies. Choose only from the **discovered role catalog** — its `shapes` are the
routing table's source of truth; a work shape the table leaves unnamed goes to the **Worker**
role. Ordinary cards route to the working roles; reviewer-lane roles receive review cards. The
card's assignee still selects the executing role — this table routes; the assignee decides.
Re-routing belongs to the card's current owner; a fresh or ambiguous card stays with its
creator.
