# Scenario — generic (any deliverable)

Refines the universal floor in `SKILL.md` and `references/review-protocol.md`; read those first.
Board operations are the `meerkat` board skill's contract.

**Applies to:** any delivered card no built-in scenario names. This is deliberately wider than
product and engineering: a legal, finance, procurement, operations, communication, incident,
data or one-off documentary delivery is audited here, as is a mixed card. When a card also
matches a built-in scenario, read that scenario too; this one never replaces it. A project
scenario refines this floor; it adds checks and leaves the six questions standing.

## Checks

Answer these six against the delivered evidence, then add the domain-specific check the
evidence suggests.

1. **Intent and authority.** Does the delivery answer the request that was actually made, for
   the requester who made it, within their authority? A substituted, narrowed or widened request
   is a finding.
2. **Completeness.** Every promised deliverable exists at the delivered sha. Omissions,
   partials and deferrals are named; nothing disappears silently.
3. **Provenance.** Every fact, number, name, date, quotation and citation traces to a source
   that exists, is current, and says what the delivery says it says. An invented or dangling
   reference is a finding.
4. **Consistency.** The artefact agrees with itself and with the project's source of truth:
   names, figures, dates, units, versions, terms. Recompute anything arithmetically checkable.
5. **Risk and irreversibility.** What does it commit — money, legal exposure, safety, data,
   reputation, time? Is the commitment within the stated authority? What is the failure mode,
   and can it be undone? Name the worst credible outcome and its owner.
6. **Fitness for the audience.** Language, register, format, sensitivity and redaction, privacy,
   and actionability: a reader can act, with an owner, a next step and a date.

Also: open questions are dispositioned, and declared constraints (deadline, budget, licence,
policy, compliance) are respected or the conflict is recorded.

## Blocks APPROVE

- A material claim with no source, or a source that does not say it.
- An omission or partial presented as complete.
- A commitment beyond the stated authority.
- A contradiction with the source of truth or within the artefact.
- A required approval, redaction or constraint missing.

## Beyond

Any check the domain's own standard, regulator, contract or counterparty calls for; record it
and let the finding block `APPROVE`.
