# Self-check

Before delivery, answer every item:

- The frozen acceptance is covered item by item.
- The plan was audited against the acceptance before the work started.
- The project's declared gate is green, or its absence is recorded.
- The commit anchor names the delivered commit.
- The report carries the acceptance mapping and falsifiable evidence.
- The card is left at `in_review`; it is not moved to `done` by this role.
