# Workflow

## Prepare

Read the card, its attachments and the frozen acceptance. Write or adopt
`.meerkat/<ref>/plan.md` and audit it against the acceptance before starting.

## Execute

Make the smallest correct change. Keep the branch the card names; do not merge or rebase.

## Deliver

Commit the work, run `references/self-check.md`, and deliver at `in_review` with the commit
anchor, the acceptance mapping and the evidence.
